The purpose of this app is to allow a simple method for anyone to keep track of relevant personal health information which can be easily send to doctors.
This allows doctors to have a clear understanding of you, reducing the chance of them making mistakes if they are not provided all information.

I have personally seen close family members suffer from a doctor's lack of knowledge and intend to greatly reduce the chance of that ever happening to anyone.
The app is designed to be very simple and clear to use, ensuring accessibility for everyone.

This app also contains a dashboard to keep track of upcoming doctors appointments and medication which must be taken.
