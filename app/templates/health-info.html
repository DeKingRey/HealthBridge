Here you can add your health information to then easily send to doctors or download for yourself.

The "Add Health Information" button brings you to a form where you can describe the details of the information you want to add.

The information may be anything, such as an allergy, a temporary injury, or a personality trait which may be relevant for doctors to know.

The "Send Health Information" button will open a prompt asking you for the email address of the desired receiver.

The "Download Health PDF" button will download your health info as an easy to read PDF which you can print off or save for later.
